## 💸 After-click revenue: what I do every day

At Unmade we build custom after-click funnels for 7 & 8-figure Shopify brands,
in three phases:

**1. Find the opportunity:** reviews, surveys, support tickets, heatmaps and
session recordings, plus a check of whether the page keeps the promise of the ad.

**2. Build the funnel:**
- Pre-sell pages, advertorials and comparison pages
- Quiz funnels
- Landing and product pages matched to the ad's message
- Cart, checkout and post-purchase flows

**3. Grow the revenue:** offer development, price experimentation, post-purchase
AOV, subscription growth, and A/B tests whenever traffic supports them.

Every test that closes, win or lose, goes into a cross-client learning library.

**200+ DTC brands · $300M+ revenue generated**
